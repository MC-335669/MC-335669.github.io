# 06 - Final Executive Review: KlarBlick AI (Germany)

## Project Overview
- **Project Slug:** `smart-glasses-de`
- **Target Market:** Germany (DACH)
- **Workflow Architecture:** Sequential Multi-Agent Pipeline (`research-agent` → `strategy-agent` → `copywriter-agent` → `developer-agent` → `qa-agent` loop → `reviewer-agent`)

## Deliverable Summary
1. **`01-research.md`:** Identified the €399–€499 mid-premium gap in Germany's 2026 smart glasses landscape and mapped DSGVO / StGB § 201 / Betriebsrat compliance barriers.
2. **`02-strategy.md`:** Established **KlarBlick AI** with a dual-SKU strategy—**KlarBlick Pure (€399, camera-free)** and **KlarBlick Pro (€499, mechanical power-cutting shutter)**—backed by local on-device AI processing.
3. **`03-copywriting.md`:** Crafted conversion-focused German B2C messaging centered on *Privacy by Design* and everyday prescription wearability.
4. **`04-prototype/index.html`:** Built a responsive German landing page prototype with interactive SKU reservation feedback.
5. **`05-qa-report.md`:** Caught and resolved German VAT disclosure (`inkl. 19 % MwSt.`), legal footer links (*Impressum* / *Datenschutzerklärung*), and battery spec alignment in a single QA loop.
